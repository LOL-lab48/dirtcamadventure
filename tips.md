# 🏍️ Dirt Bike Riding Tips

Mastering the basics makes you a safer and faster rider.

### 🛑 Smooth Braking
Don't just grab a handful of brake!
*   **Front Brake (70% power):** Squeeze smoothly with two fingers on your right hand.
*   **Rear Brake (30% power):** Press gently with your right foot to stabilize the bike on loose dirt.

### ⚙️ Shifting Gears Correctly
To shift smoothly and protect the engine:
1.  **Roll off** the throttle (twist your wrist forward to close it).
2.  **Pull in** the clutch lever on the left.
3.  **Click** the gear lever with your left foot.
4.  **Slowly let the clutch out** while gently twisting the throttle back on.

### 🦵 Squeeze the Tank
When standing up or going over bumps, squeeze the petrol tank with your knees. This holds the bike steady and stops your arms from getting tired.
