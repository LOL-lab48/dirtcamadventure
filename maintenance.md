# 🛠️ Dirt Bike Maintenance Guides

Keep your bike running like new with these basic technical checks.

### 1. Checking Your Spokes
On bikes with 17"/14" wheels, the spokes take a lot of abuse.
*   **The Test:** Take a small metal wrench and lightly tap (ping) each spoke.
*   **The Sound:** They should make a high-pitched "ding!" If it sounds dull, that spoke is loose and needs to be tightened.

### 2. Chain Care
A dry or muddy chain will snap or destroy your gears (sprockets).
*   Never let mud sit on the chain. Wash it off after your ride.
*   Spray **chain lube** on the chain while spinning the wheel before every ride.

### 3. Tyre Pressure
Dirt bikes do not use high pressure like cars or bicycles.
*   Most trail bikes run between **12 to 15 PSI**.
*   If your pressure is too high, the bike will bounce off rocks. If it is too low, you will get a flat tire!
